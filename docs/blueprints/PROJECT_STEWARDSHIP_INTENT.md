# Project stewardship and future OpenAI licensing intent

## Intent

The project owner intends, when Project Angler produces protectable work, to make it possible to grant OpenAI a direct and very broad license, with the hope of advancing beneficial AI development and attracting collaboration or sponsorship.

This record preserves project direction across context loss. It is not itself a license, assignment, offer, endorsement, sponsorship agreement, or representation that OpenAI has accepted or reviewed the project. No rights are granted by this document.

## Stewardship requirements

To preserve the ability to make a valid future grant, the project should maintain:

- a clear chain of authorship and ownership for code, documentation, inventions, datasets, evaluations, and other artifacts;
- contributor identities and explicit contribution terms before accepting outside work;
- exact provenance, versions, and licenses for third-party and recovered materials;
- separation between original project work and dependencies, model weights, datasets, trademarks, patents, or content governed by other terms;
- a dependency/license register identifying obligations, incompatibilities, attribution, source-disclosure, redistribution, patent, and termination conditions;
- evidence that imported material entered for a documented interface need and can be replaced if its terms obstruct the intended grant;
- a formal written license or assignment instrument, reviewed for the actual jurisdiction, contributors, artifact classes, patent rights, sublicensing, warranties, liability, and future work before relying on it.

## Priority constraint

Licensing, sponsorship, recognition, funding, schedule, and project continuity are subordinate to `ANG-CON-HUMAN-FLOURISHING-001`, scientific integrity, safety gates, honest evidence, and meaningful human control. No prospective sponsor—including OpenAI—may silently weaken those requirements.

## Future blueprint action

Before accepting outside contributions, public release, or a formal license grant, expand a bounded intellectual-property and stewardship blueprint with qualified legal review. Until then, describe this only as project intent.
