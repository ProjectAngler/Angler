# SAFETY capsule

Mission: enforce the Human-Flourishing Constitution while allowing bounded adaptation and preserving external authority over permissions, sealed evaluation, evidence, promotion, rollback, and deployment.

Owns threat model, human authority, permissions/resource ceilings, external dependency intake, recovered-code intake, later mutation sandbox, and bounded-evolution gate.

The learner may propose changes but cannot alter sealed tests, evidence history, its permission ceiling, promotion/deployment credentials, or external stop/rollback. SCIENCE and other branches recommend; SAFETY may veto; only human-controlled authority deploys or expands permissions.

`ANG-INV-HUMAN-FLOURISHING-001` is supreme: protect human existence, equal life/dignity/rights/agency, authentic plural well-being, truth, human authority, and corrigibility. Happiness is voluntary and human-defined, never a scalar justification for coercion, manipulation, surveillance, forced conformity, or aggregate sacrifice.

Every promotable artifact requires `ANG-CTR-HUMAN-IMPACT-ASSESSMENT-001` and `ANG-GATE-HUMAN-FLOURISHING-001`. High-impact proposals need independent human review; the learner cannot approve or amend its own constitution or assessment.

Required children: THREAT-MODEL, HUMAN-AUTHORITY, PERMISSIONS, DEPENDENCY-INTAKE, RECOVERED-CODE-INTAKE, MUTATION-SANDBOX, EVOLUTION-GATE.

External and recovered code require a demonstrated interface need, exact version/license, fit and trust analysis, tests, modification boundary, maintenance plan, and exit path. Recovery gives no architectural privilege.

THREAT-MODEL, HUMAN-AUTHORITY, and PERMISSIONS now have CR0 design packages. `ANG-POL-LOCAL-SCAFFOLD-001` plus `ANG-ASSESS-CONSTRUCTION-RELEASE-0-001` authorizes LOW, human-directed `BOOTSTRAP_WORK` only: exact local paths, synthetic data, named offline PowerShell/Python validators, numeric ceilings, and exact rollback. Model/GPU work, training, network, packages/tools, recovered outputs, real-person data, out-of-scope files, background services, deployment/external effects, autonomous continuation, and self-modification are denied.

Bootstrap `ALLOW` is not promotion and does not pass `ANG-GATE-HUMAN-FLOURISHING-001`, Slice 00, M0, `ANG-GATE-SAFETY-DESIGN-001`, or a scientific/technical gate. The learner cannot write or approve any policy, assessment, evidence, gate, permission, or resume decision.

Current gates: `ANG-GATE-CONSTRUCTION-RELEASE-0-001` passed for bootstrap design; `ANG-GATE-SAFETY-DESIGN-001` and `ANG-GATE-HUMAN-FLOURISHING-001` remain open. Design revision 2 draft; delivery documented only for CR0 governance.

Top risks: authority leakage, sealed-data exposure, paternalistic “protection,” aggregate sacrifice, manipulative “happiness,” moral paralysis, supply-chain compromise, self-deployment, and documentation-only controls.

Next action: activate one human-directed ready CR0 leaf whose exact paths, commands, executor, synthetic fixtures, resource/output ceilings, stop conditions, expiry, and rollback are narrower than ADR-0002 and the local-scaffold policy. Dependency/recovered-code intake remains deferred because CR0 permits neither.
