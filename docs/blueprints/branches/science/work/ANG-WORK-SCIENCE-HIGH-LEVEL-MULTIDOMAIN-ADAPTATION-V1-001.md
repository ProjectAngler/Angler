- derive and validate identities, partitions, budgets, aliases, and canonical

- strictly parse bounded model output into a public answer/action sequence;

- keep evaluator-only solutions and final payloads outside model, learner,
  prompt, recall, and current-turn feedback inputs;

  resources, and publish immutable evidence; and

keyword-to-procedure rule, verifier-derived plan, candidate ranker based on task

or threshold chosen after results. Objective judges may say whether a frozen
submission succeeded but expose only scalar disposition and generic feedback.

immutable Qwen generation record immediately after generation and before strict
proposal parsing. A parser rejection then leaves the exact raw response/token
evidence available while still returning no candidate and permitting no retry,
repair, selection, or execution. This is evidence retention only; generation,
parsing, and cycle semantics are otherwise unchanged.

Qwen supplies every procedure proposal and final response. The full arm's
learned owner supplies selection. The retrieval-only control uses the literal
first Qwen proposal under a preregistered nonsemantic policy; this is control
plumbing, not a solver. No parser may add, reorder, infer, or repair an action.

## Task families and public formats

The exact evaluator wrapper is
`experiments/evaluators/high_level_multidomain_v1.py`. It creates one
evaluator-owned process that retains hidden solutions and releases public tasks
only for the authorized phase. Its canonical suite schema is
`angler.high-level-multidomain.v1`.

1. **Symbolic demonstration transfer.** Adaptation items expose two raw
   five-symbol input/output demonstrations. Development/final items use fresh
   symbols and omit demonstrations while retaining the same hidden procedure
   within a replicate. The response is exactly five listed public symbols,
   comma-separated. The hidden judge uses
   `score_demonstration_procedure_answer`; exact success is score `1.0`, and
   pairwise agreement is retained as a secondary metric.
2. **Opaque glyph-machine planning.** Adaptation items expose public transition
   traces; development/final items use trace-free origin/goal encounters under
   the same evaluator-private dynamics within a replicate. States and
   zero-argument actions receive task-local public aliases. The response is a
   comma-separated action-alias sequence ending in `STOP`, within the public
   step ceiling. The hidden judge commits the parsed sequence and calls
   `judge_glyph_procedure_attempt`; terminal exactness is the primary score.
3. **Causal operator execution.** Token, file, and box worlds expose only
   declarative origin, goal, permitted action schema, task-local entity/location
   aliases, and a two- or four-step ceiling. The response is a semicolon-
   separated bounded action list in the public grammar. The judge calls
   `commit_action_sequence` and `evaluate_committed_sequence`; no route is
   precomputed or returned to the learner.

Malformed, out-of-vocabulary, duplicate-STOP, overlength, or trailing-content
responses receive objective score zero. The raw response remains preserved;
the parser performs no repair.

rerun. A valid result is `EXPERIMENTALLY_SUPPORTED` only if every predicate is
