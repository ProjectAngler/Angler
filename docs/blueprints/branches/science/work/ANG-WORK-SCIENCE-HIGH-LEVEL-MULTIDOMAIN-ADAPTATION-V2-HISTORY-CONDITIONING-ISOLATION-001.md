## Shadow-only prospective congruence witness

Each finalized attempt emits a leaf-local
`ProcedureOutcomeCongruenceWitnessV1` after objective judgment:

- `UNKNOWN` only when a required artifact is absent, as for a malformed
  unadmitted proposal or an earlier integrity stop;

  grammar, and the objective judgment is success; and
- `VIOLATED` when the required artifacts exist but any selected/executed/

  false, or objective success is false.

or affect any attempt/result classification. `VIOLATED` does not prove that
the selected natural-language procedure was semantically disobeyed.
