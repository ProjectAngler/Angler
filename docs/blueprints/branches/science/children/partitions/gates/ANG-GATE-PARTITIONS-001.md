# Partition design gate

- Gate: `ANG-GATE-PARTITIONS-001@1`
- Pass: all six visibility classes are defined; candidate freeze precedes sealed access; unauthorized payload resolution, deliberate overlap, and leaked-seed canaries are rejected 100%; exposure retires material.
- Evidence: schema/access/overlap test receipt and content identities.
- Failure: quarantine and rotate the suite; invalidate dependent receipts.
- Waiver: none after adaptive results are viewed.
- Human impact: bootstrap policy only; fixtures cannot become learner data without a new assessment.
