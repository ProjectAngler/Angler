# WORLDS design gate

The design is construction-ready when reset/step/score semantics, identities, visibility, budgets, permissions, typed failures, cleanup, outcome-only feedback, and replay tolerances are versioned; two initial families and their independent transformation requirements are named; and generated/adaptive curricula remain deferred.

CR0 may build schemas and synthetic fixtures only. No executable environment, model call, generated code, hidden evaluation payload, or learner data is permitted. Failure leaves the branch unready and retires affected schema identities. Bootstrap authorization is not a flourishing-gate, Slice 00, or M0 pass.
