## Accountable outcome

The consumed V11 observation localized a real bottleneck without granting a
terminal claim: its graph codes remained nearly collinear (cosine
0.9963–0.9973), graph-address variation was about forty times smaller than the
pooled-semantic address variation, and graph removal did not reduce 4/8
behavior. V10 already proved that bounded allocation can retain 48 distinct
events. V12 therefore removes memory entirely and asks whether a scaled OML
objective can learn a non-collapsed natural-language procedure representation
that transfers to unseen topology families.

A corpus audit found that V6's apparent 384 training episodes contain only 134
unique trace strings across sixteen families. Reorder, omission, bypass, and
challenge insertion occupy fixed patterns. V12 must increase compositional and
surface diversity rather than repeat those examples or tune graph-logit scale.

## Frozen OML protocol

Use exactly 192 owner updates, eight inner steps, eight outer mechanisms,
inner PLN learning rate `1e-3`, outer RLN AdamW learning rate `3e-4`, betas
`(0.9,0.999)`, epsilon `1e-8`, zero weight decay, gradient clip `5.0`, and the
V20 robust objective `0.5*mean + 0.5*0.05*logmeanexp(loss/0.05)`. No early
stop, seed retry, replay rescue, result-dependent threshold, extra epoch,
autocast, TF32, model update, or final access is permitted.

Development uses four frozen panels over the twelve unseen families. Each
panel adapts on public outcome episodes from one mechanism and probes a disjoint
mechanism, with both same-transition and cross-transition pairings. Evaluate
second-order online/no-update, first-order online/no-update, source online/no-
update, shuffled-outcome adaptation, direction removed, and step-semantics
removed. Report families and transition types separately.

The first complete development result passes only if all are true:

- second-order online loss AUC is below first-order, at most 0.95 times it,
  below source-online and second-order no-update, improves at least 3/4 panels,
  and has no panel regression;
- second-order no-update balanced accuracy is at least 0.75 and at least 0.10
  above source no-update, establishing slow-representation credit separately
  from fast adaptation;
- true-outcome adaptation beats shuffled outcomes; direction removal and
  step-semantics removal each lose at least 0.05 balanced accuracy or the
  preregistered equivalent loss margin;
- centered 32-D development codes have effective rank at least 8 and finite
  nonzero per-dimension variance; raw normalized codes have mean off-diagonal
  cosine at most 0.95 and at most 10% of distinct pairs above 0.999; and
  same-multiset reorder separation is at least 0.05 beyond same-procedure
  paraphrase distance. These operands are frozen separately so centering cannot
  manufacture apparent relief from V11-style common-direction collapse;
- at least 9/12 unseen families improve over source and each transition class
  improves in at least 2/3 families; no aggregate may hide a collapsed class;
- padding/repeat invariance, paired starts/data, full-vs-split outer gradients,
  fast-head-only inner mutation, RLN-only outer mutation, final seal, source
  hashes, and frozen Qwen digest are exact.

If loss improves while code-collapse gates fail, classify it as a shortcut and
do not integrate. Accept the first result without tuning. Only a full
development pass may open final. Only a supported final RLN may later be loaded
strict/read-only into a new V10-memory integration identity; V11 is never
resumed or tuned.
